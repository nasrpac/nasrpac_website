    04 / Our story

    From pioneer
    to leader.

    1. 1973

      Founded in 1973 by the late Mr. Mohammed Mustafa Abed, Nasrpac began as one of the Kingdom's carton-converting pioneers — serving the growing demand for packaging across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

    2. 50+ yrs

      Five decades of experience in the carton family business. Continuous investment in technology has built a trusted name in the printing and converting industry.

    3. Today

      Today, family-driven leadership continues the founder's vision. Engr. Taleb Abed (Management) drives strategic vision and operational modernisation; Mr. Ahmed Abed (Finance) stewards long-term investment capacity.
